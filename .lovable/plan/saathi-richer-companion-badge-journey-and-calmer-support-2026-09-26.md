# SAATHI: richer companion, badge journey, and calmer Support

## Goal
Make the existing mobile prototype feel more alive without changing its navigation or introducing real AI or a backend. Keep the current SAATHI identity, but use fewer competing colours on Support and make the companion's motion unmistakable yet gentle.

## Experience changes
1. **Journey and collection:** Show the same illustrated collectible badges on their corresponding journey milestones instead of generic milestone icons. Expand the collection from six to approximately 30 thoughtfully named badges across self-care, connection, and community. Give each a distinctive illustrated treatment within a coherent family, a specific achievable requirement, earned/locked appearance, progress, and details. Group or progressively reveal the larger collection so Profile stays easy to browse. Award badges only for actual local prototype actions; no badges for feeling “better,” streak pressure, or distress scores.
2. **Check-in celebration:** Make the warmth symbol and number visibly animate when a *new day's* check-in is completed: a short, satisfying rise/flare, number transition, soft glow, and a few particles, coordinated with the companion and any new badge. Do not copy Duolingo's fire icon, shape, or animation. Reopening the check-in the same day must not increment or replay an unlock. Provide an equivalent readable static state when reduced motion is preferred.
3. **Companion:** Evolve the existing SVG cat into a clearly articulated, expressive character rather than replacing it with a static image. Animate its face, ears, paws, tail, posture, and gaze with state-specific motion for idle, curious, listening, thinking, supportive, happy, and celebrating. Keep one recognizable design on Home, check-in, journey moments, and chat; especially on difficult days it remains calm and nonjudgmental. Avoid constant large movement while reading.
4. **Chat companion:** Put that same animated cat in the SAATHI conversation header/identity and alongside its messages and typing state. Map prototype events to expressions: opening/greeting, composing, message sent, waiting/thinking, supportive reply, human-support invitation, and completion/positive response. Keep the existing local mock replies and history; structure expression mapping so later real chat responses can drive the same states without claiming current AI understanding. Never infer clinical state from message text.
5. **Support:** Simplify the existing Support screen to a quiet, more consistent palette and clearer hierarchy: listener, counsellor, requests, and emergency assistance remain accessible, with emergency still unmistakable but not visually loud. Preserve existing mock actions, honest role labels, and prototype-only safety language.

## Technical approach
- Keep the single-route, local-only prototype and existing Motion library. Centralize expanded badge definitions, categories, thresholds, and eligibility in the mock-data layer; reuse badge artwork in both journey and collection. Keep the original six recognizable.
- Use Motion and animated SVG parts for the cat and warmth sequence, with reduced-motion fallbacks and restrained one-shot effects. No new animation library unless a concrete limitation is found.
- Keep check-in counting to one per local calendar day and derive badge eligibility from the existing local activity. Do not fabricate earned dates for new badges. Avoid unlocking repeated badges whenever Profile is merely reopened.
- Verify the new-day check-in → warmth/companion celebration → journey badge flow, same-day repeat, badge filters/details, Support actions, and chat expression transitions on narrow and desktop viewports, including reduced motion.
